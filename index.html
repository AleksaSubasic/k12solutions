The week

Seven days, three moves.

01

Day 1 — Brief

A 45-minute call, then a one-page scope: sitemap, features, what copy and photos you owe us. You approve it and we start. No proposal PDF, no second meeting.

02

Days 2–5 — Build

Design and code happen together on a live staging link you can open any time. You leave comments on the page itself; we clear them daily. Mobile is built first, not last.

03

Days 6–7 — Ship

Performance pass, forms tested, analytics and technical SEO wired, DNS moved. You get the logins and a short video walkthrough of how to edit it yourself.

Cheap is a schedule, not a shortcut.

Agencies bill for the weeks between the work. We removed the weeks. What's left is the same code, the same care, at a price a five-person business can sign off in one meeting.

01 / Fixed price

The number in the quote is the number on the invoice. Scope changes get their own quote before anyone starts typing.

02 / You own it

Your domain, your hosting, your admin account, standard tooling. Leave whenever you like — nothing is locked to us.

03 / Two of us, no juniors

The people on the call are the people writing the code. That is the whole reason a week is enough.

01 — The problem

Bookings died at the phone line

Their front desk is staffed by whoever is not currently assisting a procedure. Calls between 9 and 17 went unanswered perhaps a third of the time, and the callers — mostly people in pain, deciding fast — rang the next clinic on the list. Half their prospective patients were also expats searching in English. Everything they had was in Serbian only.

02 — The approach

What we decided to do about it.

Scope

One page, ruthlessly

Six days meant one page. Treatments, prices, the two dentists, directions, and a booking widget. Everything else was cut and has not been missed.

Language

English is not an afterthought

Both languages built at once with a switch in the header, and both indexed properly. English pages now bring in a third of new bookings.

Booking

Into the calendar they already use

No new system to learn. The widget writes into the calendar the front desk already had open all day.

03 — Zero to live

The whole build, in order.

Day 1

Brief

One call. Prices and treatment list arrived as a photo of a laminated sheet, which was fine.

Days 2–3

Build

Single page, both languages, mobile-first. Copy written by us from the call and reviewed the same evening.

Day 4

Booking integration

Calendar connected, buffer times and treatment durations set, confirmation and reminder emails written in both languages.

Day 5

Review

Both dentists went through it on their phones and left nine comments. All cleared the same day.

Day 6

Launch

DNS, Google Business, schema markup for a local medical practice, and the phone number made tappable everywhere.

04 — Two calls worth explaining

Where we pushed back.

Prices are on the page

Clinics almost never publish prices. We pushed hard for it — the number one question on the phone was cost, and hiding it was filtering out nobody except the people who would have booked. Enquiries dropped in volume and rose sharply in quality.

[ treatment + price list — 1600×900 ]

Reminders, not just bookings

The booking widget was the request. The automated 24-hour SMS reminder was our addition, and it is the thing that actually moved money: no-shows fell by roughly 40% in the first quarter.

[ reminder email + SMS — 1600×900 ]
